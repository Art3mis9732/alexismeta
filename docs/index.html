    How to read this table
    Right side after 4h: how often price was on the pattern's side of the entry four hours later (50% = coin flip). After fees: average result per trade in units of risk (R) with the stop at the pattern's own invalidation, target 1.5× the risk, entry at the next candle's open, 0.06% taker fee each way plus slippage. −0.50R means you lose half your risked amount per trade on average. Later 30%: the same number on the most recent 30% of the data only — if it flips sign, the result is not stable. 100× wiped: how often price moved far enough against the trade within 4 hours to liquidate a 100× position. With this many patterns and three coins, a few rows will look good by luck alone.
