## 4. Limitations
Margin/leverage are the simulator's 2× convention, not Bitget margin tiers; no margin add/remove; unsettled funding is an estimate; timeline and drawdown exist only from the day recording started (the forward worker records from 11:11 UTC); latency was measured on loopback in the cloud container, not on Windows or over a network; the legacy replay needs the page to hold candles back to the trade's entry (INCOMPLETE otherwise); the cloud worker still dies with the session, so continuous monitoring is not claimed.
